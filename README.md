# Top Relationship Intelligence & Mapping Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Network Graph Analytics, Warm Introduction Paths & CRM Data Enrichment*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Relationship Intelligence & Mapping Tools**. These tools automatically analyze email, calendar, and CRM data to map professional networks, score relationship strength, and surface warm introduction paths — helping sales, partnerships, and business development teams leverage collective relationship capital.

**Examples** include Salesforce Einstein Relationship Insights, Introhive, Affinity, Relationship Science (RelSci), Nektar.ai, BoardEx, DealCloud, Leadspace, ZoomInfo Chorus, and Altrata (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom relationship graphs, and transparent contact intelligence — ideal for revenue operations teams, data engineers, and developers building relationship-aware applications.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Einstein Relationship Insights](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  
  AI-powered relationship discovery within Salesforce that searches web, news, and Salesforce files to surface connections and relationship graphs. Pricing starts at $50/user/month for Starter and $150/user/month for standard edition .

- **[Introhive](https://www.introhive.com/)**  
  AI-powered Relationship Intelligence platform for B2B and professional services firms. Automatically maps and scores relationships from email/calendar data, delivering 90% contact data accuracy and 30% increase in referrals for users .

- **[Affinity](https://www.affinity.co/)**  
  Relationship intelligence CRM for relationship-driven industries (PE, VC, banking). Automatically builds network maps from email/calendar data, scores relationship strength, and surfaces warm introduction paths via browser extension .

- **[Relationship Science (RelSci)](https://relsci.com/)**  
  Relationship mapping platform with deep information on 6+ million influential decision makers. Features Path Finder for mapping connections between entities, 50+ search filters, and API integration for CRM access .

- **[Nektar.ai](https://nektar.ai/)**  
  Revenue data foundation that automatically captures email/meeting interactions, maps buying committees, and writes clean data into CRM. Claims ~80% of emails/meetings never reach CRM without automation; processes with >97% entity matching accuracy .

- **[BoardEx](https://www.boardex.com/)**  
  Executive intelligence and relationship mapping platform covering ~2 million organizations and 1.6 million leaders. Provides detailed career histories, board connections, and relationship paths for executive search and business development .

- **[DealCloud](https://www.dealcloud.com/)**  
  Deal and relationship management platform for financial services with Relationship Intelligence API for importing email/meeting data into scoring workflows. Supports automated contact creation and relationship path mapping .

- **[Leadspace](https://www.leadspace.com/)**  
  B2B customer data platform with GTM Data Intelligence Cloud for unified buyer intelligence, buying group identification, and real-time signal activation across marketing, sales, and RevOps .

- **[ZoomInfo Chorus](https://www.zoominfo.com/)**  
  Conversation intelligence platform with relationship intelligence capabilities — captures and syncs contacts and communications, providing complete deal context and Momentum Insights .

- **[Altrata](https://altrata.com/)**  
  Umbrella entity including BoardEx, RelSci, WealthEngine, and Wealth-X, delivering data and intelligence on influential individuals and organizations .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Kindred Keeper](https://github.com/iwaheedsattar/kindred-keeper)**  
  Private, local-first relationship memory desk for thoughtful follow-ups. Tracks contacts, analyzes notes for interests/promises/dates, scores relationship warmth, and generates weekly check-in plans. Runs entirely on your computer with Python standard library — no account, no telemetry, no hosted database . MIT licensed.

- **[Relationship Substrate](https://github.com/dbmcco/relationship-substrate)**  
  Source module for owning relationship records with typed read/correction surfaces. Exposes person, organization, affiliation, interaction, and subject_note record kinds. Includes agent-callable tool surfaces for relationship search, history-backed people search, and operating picture generation . Designed for agent integration rather than human UI.

- **[Neo4j Community](https://github.com/neo4j/neo4j)**  
  Leading open-source graph database for modeling professional networks, relationships, and path-finding queries. Cypher query language enables efficient traversal of relationship graphs. GPL-3.0 licensed.

- **[Apache AGE](https://github.com/apache/age)**  
  PostgreSQL extension adding graph database functionality. Enables relationship network queries directly within PostgreSQL, ideal for teams already invested in the Postgres ecosystem. Apache 2.0 licensed.

- **[Memgraph](https://github.com/memgraph/memgraph)**  
  Open-source in-memory graph database compatible with Cypher, optimized for real-time relationship analytics and network traversal. BSL licensed.

- **[NebulaGraph](https://github.com/vesoft-inc/nebula)**  
  Open-source distributed graph database handling billions of vertices and edges. Suitable for large-scale relationship network analysis across organizations. Apache 2.0 licensed.

- **[NetworkX](https://github.com/networkx/networkx)**  
  Python library for creating, manipulating, and analyzing complex networks. Implements graph algorithms for centrality, community detection, and shortest paths — essential for relationship strength scoring and warm path identification. BSD-3-Clause licensed.

- **[Cytoscape.js](https://github.com/cytoscape/cytoscape.js)**  
  Open-source graph theory library for visualizing relationship networks in web applications. Supports interactive network exploration and path highlighting. MIT licensed.

- **[Sigma.js](https://github.com/jacomyal/sigma.js)**  
  Open-source JavaScript library for graph drawing and network visualization. Optimized for rendering large relationship graphs in browsers. MIT licensed.

- **[Gephi](https://github.com/gephi/gephi)**  
  Open-source network visualization and exploration tool for relationship graphs and social networks. Desktop application for interactive network analysis. GPL-3.0 licensed.

- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  
  Modern open-source CRM with contacts, companies, and opportunities management. Provides a foundation for building relationship intelligence features on top of a flexible data model. AGPL-3.0 licensed.

- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  
  Open-source CRM with customizable entities, workflow automation, and relationship tracking. Extensible for building custom relationship mapping and scoring features. GPL-3.0 licensed.

- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  
  Open-source CRM with relationship management, contact tracking, and interaction history. Can be extended with graph database integrations for relationship intelligence. AGPL-3.0 licensed.

- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  
  Open-source Laravel CRM with lead management, pipeline tracking, and email integration. Suitable foundation for relationship intelligence in small to mid-size teams. MIT licensed.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[Dgraph](https://github.com/dgraph-io/dgraph)** — GraphQL-native graph database for large-scale relationship mapping with distributed architecture. Apache 2.0 licensed.
- **[ArangoDB](https://github.com/arangodb/arangodb)** — Multi-model database with graph, document, and key-value capabilities for flexible relationship modeling. Apache 2.0 licensed.
- **[JanusGraph](https://github.com/JanusGraph/janusgraph)** — Distributed graph database with pluggable storage backends for massive relationship networks. Apache 2.0 licensed.
- **[Apache TinkerPop](https://github.com/apache/tinkerpop)** — Graph computing framework with Gremlin query language for relationship traversal. Apache 2.0 licensed.
- **[igraph](https://github.com/igraph/igraph)** — Network analysis library with R, Python, and C interfaces for relationship modeling. GPL-2.0 licensed.
- **[Graphology](https://github.com/graphology/graphology)** — JavaScript/TypeScript graph data structures and analytics for browser and Node.js relationship apps. MIT licensed.
- **[vis.js Network](https://github.com/visjs/vis-network)** — Interactive network visualization library for relationship graphs. Apache 2.0 licensed.
- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)** — Open-source BI with network visualization for relationship analytics dashboards. AGPL-3.0 licensed.
- **Entity Resolution Tools** — Dedupe, Splink, Zingg for matching contacts across sources.
- **Graph ML Libraries** — PyTorch Geometric, DGL for link prediction and relationship scoring.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Neo4j** or **NebulaGraph** for the relationship graph, **NetworkX** for analysis, **Cytoscape.js** or **Sigma.js** for visualization, **Twenty** or **EspoCRM** for CRM data, and **Apache Superset** for dashboards to build a complete self-hosted relationship intelligence stack.
